# FM-1 controls · MDMA 0.5.6-controls

**Play your first groove.** Press HOME until LIST appears. Turn PRESETS to choose a city. Starting with all layers off, press PLAY/STOP to bring in drums and bass. Press the second black key to select the GROOVE scene. Turn MASTER to set the overall volume.

## Stage views and keyboard

HOME cycles through **LIST → MAP → MIXER → LIST**. From another page, HOME returns to LIST. Turn SELECT in any stage view to choose a layer while the groove plays. Look for the selection marker at the bottom left of LIST or the top left of MAP and MIXER.

| Control | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Black keys 1–4, from the left | Select INTRO, GROOVE, TENSION or PEAK for the current city. |
| Black keys 5–10 | Toggle DRUMS, BASS, AMB, PLUCK, LEAD and SPARKS, in that order. On stage, the key also selects that layer. In EDIT and SEQ, you can toggle its sound while continuing to edit the same layer. |
| Black key 11 | Silence the active layers. Press again to recall that combination. If only DRUMS was on, only DRUMS returns. Layers with VOL at zero stay silent. |
| White keys | Play the selected layer in the city's scale. In SCL they select the root note. In the drum sequencer they edit steps. |
| PLAY/STOP | Toggle DRUMS and BASS together. If only DRUMS is on, pressing it turns DRUMS off and BASS on. |
| PRESETS | Choose a city. During playback the change is queued. In GLO and SAVE the selection takes effect immediately. |
| ALGORITHM | Cycle through eight variations of the selected layer on stage or in SEQ. |
| Hold EDIT and turn PRESETS | On stage, borrow the selected layer from another city. Hold EDIT briefly before turning. |
| LFO / ENV | On stage, hold LFO to solo the selected layer or ENV to mute it. Release to hear the full mix again. |
| OCT− / OCT+ | On stage, in SCL or in SEQ, tap to change tempo by 1 BPM. Hold to sweep toward 60 or 240 BPM. BPM RELEASE in GLO determines what happens on release. |
| REC | In MIXER, toggle encoder measurement. The four knobs register movement during measurement. Press REC again to resume sound adjustments. |
| ARP | Unassigned in this version. |

## MIX, FX and EQ

On stage, press FX to cycle through the three knob banks in the current view. HOME keeps the selected bank when you switch views. From another page, FX opens LIST with the effects bank selected. Each bank adjusts the selected layer.

| Bank | Knob 1 | Knob 2 | Knob 3 | Knob 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| MIX | VOL · volume | PAN · stereo position | FLT · filter | RES · resonance |
| FX | REV · reverb | DLY · delay | PHS · phaser | SAT · saturation |
| EQ | LO · lows | LO-MID · low mids | HI-MID · high mids | HI · highs |

Turn slowly for finer MIX and FX adjustments or quickly to cover more of the range. EQ moves in 1 dB steps from −9 to +6 dB. To check the low end of BASS, hold LFO as you adjust LO, then release to hear it with the drums.

## EDIT · tone controls

Tap EDIT to open the parameters. Tap again to return to LIST. Use OCT− or OCT+ to switch layers. Each knob adjusts one row. Turn SELECT to highlight a row and ALGORITHM to switch its mode. You can also hold EDIT and turn SELECT to switch layers.

| Layer | Knob 1 | Knob 2 | Knob 3 | Knob 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DRUMS | TONE | DECAY · duration | PUNCH · attack | DRIVE · distortion |
| BASS | CUTOFF · filter cutoff | RES · resonance | ENV AMT · envelope depth | ENV DECAY · envelope decay |
| AMB | CUTOFF | MIST · texture | DRIFT · movement | FADE · transition |
| PLUCK | CUTOFF | RES | DECAY | DETUNE |
| LEAD | CUTOFF | RES | GLIDE · slide between notes | GHOST RATE · ghost speed |
| SPARKS | CUTOFF | RES | DECAY | SWEEP |

## SEQ · steps and fills

- **Queue a live fill.** With DRUMS playing in LIST, MAP or MIXER, hold SEQ for about 0.35 seconds. FILL NEXT means the fill is queued. Hold again before it starts to cancel. FILL IN appears while it plays.
- **Open the grid.** Tap SEQ. Knob 4 selects the layer. In DRUMS, SELECT chooses KICK, HAT, OPEN, CLAP, PERC or NOISE. On other layers, SELECT cycles through layers. Tap SEQ to leave.
- **Edit a drum step.** The 16 white keys correspond to the 16 steps. Tap a key to toggle its step in the selected lane. Hold it for about 0.3 seconds and turn knobs 1–4 to adjust VEL (velocity), TIME (timing offset), ATK (attack) or REL (release). Editing an inactive step turns it on.
- **Try a variation.** With DRUMS selected and the keys released, turn knob 1 to draft a HAT and PERC variation. Turn knob 2 clockwise to preview. Turn knob 3 clockwise to apply. Turn knob 2 or 3 counterclockwise to discard. Leaving SEQ also clears the draft.

## SCL · scale

Press the SEL button to open SCL. Turn the SELECT encoder or knob 1 to choose the root note. The first 12 white keys also select the 12 roots, starting at A. ALGORITHM or knob 2 switches between major and minor. Press SEL again to return to LIST.

## GLO · tempo, modes and cities

Press GLO to open or close the page. Turn SELECT to choose a row. For settings, turn ALGORITHM or press OCT−/+ to change the value. Knobs 1–4 adjust the first four rows directly.

| Row | Setting |
| --- | --- |
| BPM RELEASE | AUTO returns to the starting tempo when you release OCT after a sweep. MANUAL keeps the tempo reached. |
| SWING EVERYTHING | Set the city's swing. |
| BASS MODE / LEAD MODE | Switch the corresponding layer between HOLD and ALIEN. |
| GENERATE RANDOM CITY | Create a city. The device holds up to eight. |
| DELETE CURRENT CITY | Delete the selected generated city. The factory cities Búzios, Rio and Ibiza are protected. |
| FACTORY RESET | Use ALGORITHM to choose CURRENT CITY or ALL CITIES. Check the target before confirming. |
| CLOCK | Select internal timing or external sync over USB or TRS. |

To generate, delete or reset, press OCT+ to open the confirmation. Press OCT+ again to proceed or OCT− to cancel.

## SAVE · store and recall

Press SAVE. Turn SELECT to choose a row and PRESETS to choose the city. Check the name on screen. Press OCT+ twice to confirm, or OCT− to cancel. Press SAVE to return to LIST.

| Row | Action |
| --- | --- |
| SAVE TO DEVICE | Write to device storage. Turn ALGORITHM to choose the current city or all cities. |
| POINT | Store a copy of the city in the single return point. When occupied, use ALGORITHM to choose SAVE or LOAD. |
| DELETE POINT | Clear the return point. The main device save is stored separately. |

**Try this at rehearsal.** Save the city to POINT before experimenting. If you like the changes, use SAVE TO DEVICE. To recall the earlier copy, choose POINT → LOAD.
